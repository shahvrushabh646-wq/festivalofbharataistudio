# Festival of Bharat AI Studio V51–V70

V70 is a consolidated Creator Autopilot layer built on the V30–V50 master workflow.

## V51–55 — Intelligence
- Trend radar input and trend-pattern planning
- content-gap analysis from the user's own history
- topic clusters and freshness signals
- competitor/content-reference notes without copying content

## V56–60 — Creative Director
- four story strategies
- shot-by-shot storyboard
- shot demand list
- footage search query generation
- asset scoring and diversity allocation

## V61–65 — Production
- pacing and duration guidance
- caption/cover/audio direction
- 4 distinct Reel variants
- quality checklist
- rights and cultural-safety gates

## V66–69 — Learning
- performance memory
- A/B creative planning
- self-re-edit recommendations
- content calendar and feedback loop

## V70 — Full AI Creator Autopilot
Trend → Idea → Script → Storyboard → Footage → Rights → Edit → Quality → Re-edit → Caption/Cover → 4 Reels → Approval → Analytics → Learning.

### Important limits
This free build is a local/browser orchestration layer. It does not claim unlimited free cloud AI video generation, live Instagram analytics without a connection, or automatic Instagram publishing. External footage must be checked for its specific license and any applicable privacy/personality restrictions. Human approval stays enabled.
